Build 1.0.3725.0. Read with CodeWalker 30 dev46 (CodeWalker.Core through a scratch script, MetaXml for PSO/RBF/resources). Mods folder skipped: these are the vanilla archives. All rows below are **Verified** (read in the files) unless marked "our reading".

## PHY-001: the two unnamed `materials.dat` columns

Aligning the header with tab size 4 (as the file asks) puts the 0.95 column under `SOFTNESS` and the 0.0 column under `NOISINES` (sic). The file's comment header defines both: SOFTNESS "how much you can sink into the material (used for second surface)", NOISINESS "how loud the material is (used by the stealth code)". The header also lists `VIBRATION`, which has no column any more (the rumble profile column took its place). All 213 rows in the update copy hold 0.95 / 0.0, so in this build neither column varies per material. Order after `TOP_SPEED_MULT`: SOFTNESS, NOISINESS, PENETRATION_RESISTANCE, then flags SEE_THRU, SHOOT_THRU, SHOOT_THRU_FX, NO_DECAL, then POROUS, HEATS_TYRE, then the audio material.

## ENT-019: what `nm_blend_out_sets.meta` sets

`CNmBlendOutSetManager` with 103 sets (`NMBS_*`; same names in base and update, update copy changed values; `NMBS_PLAYER_MP_ACTION_MODE_CLONE_GETUPS` appears twice). A set is a small graph of items, each with a `type`, an `id` and a `nextItemId`. ## ENT-020: where fall damage is set

Not in the data files. A search of all 2,134 extracted data files (every `.meta`, `.ymt`, `.dat`, `.xml` under `common\data`, `update\common\data`, `update\x64\data`, plus DLC copies) for any field combining fall/land/impact with damage/health finds none; the only damage-like matches are vehicle `fCollisionDamageMult` and weapon damage fall-off. What the files do set is when a fall becomes a ragdoll: `playerinfo.ymt` gives every `PlayerStatInfos` entry `MinFallHeight` 6 and `MaxFallHeight` 8.5 (the guide only quotes 6), and `physicstasks.ymt` sets the high-fall and catch-fall thresholds (`FallingSpeedForHighFall` 5 and 4 in different blocks, `RollUpHeightThreshold` 5, `CatchFallHeightThresholdRollUp` 3, `CatchFallHeightThresholdWindmill` 1, `CatchFallHeightThresholdClipPose` 10, `TimeToStartCatchFall` 2000 ms, `TimeToStartCatchFallPlayer` 500 ms). `pedhealth.meta` (10 health configs) holds only health and armour thresholds (Default, Fatigued, Hurt, Injured, Dying, DogTakedown, MeleeCardinalFatalAttackCheck, Invincible). Conclusion: fall damage is computed in the engine, so the row moves to Part 2 or an in-game test. Status: partly (data side ruled out).

## AI-014: `taskdata.meta` and `scriptedguntasks.meta`

`taskdata.meta` (`CTaskDataInfoManager`, update copy, 10 sets, default `STANDARD_PED`): per ped type, the wander anim group (`WANDER` or `WANDER_ANIMAL`), `ScenarioAttractionDistance` (10 m for STANDARD_PED and Fish, 30 m for the other animals), `SurfaceSwimmingDepthOffset` 0.9, `SwimmingWanderPointRange` 10, and a flag list (45 distinct flags). Sets: STANDARD_PED (5 flags: PreferFleeOnPavements, CanScreamDuringFlee, CanBeShoved, CanBeTalkedTo, CanShove), Animal (20), Dog (24), Cat (22), Fish (22), KillerWhale (22), LargeFish (21), TigerShark (21), Whale (22), Stingray (23). Our reading: these are the names a script passes (as a hash) to `TASK_AIM_GUN_SCRIPTED`; usage not counted in the decompile yet.

## AI-033: how decision makers map events to responses

`events.meta` (update copy) has three parts.
1. `eventResponseTaskData`: 40 response tasks (`RESPONSE_TASK_FLEE`, `_COMBAT`, `_THREAT`, `_COWER`, `_HANDS_UP`, `_TURN_TO_FACE`, `RESPONSE_POLICE_TASK_WANTED` ...), a few with a parameter (`Flee`, `Face`, `TimeToCrouch` 3 s).
2. `eventDecisionMakerResponseData`: response blocks (223 in our count; the guide says 219, recount before quoting), each for one `Event` (74 distinct event names) with an ordered `Decision` list. Each decision (461 in all) names a `TaskRef`, four chances by who caused the event (`Chance_SourcePlayer`, `_SourceFriend`, `_SourceThreat`, `_SourceOther`; values 1.0 about 1,350 times, 0.0 about 475, 0.5 14, 0.4 2), a squared distance window (`DistanceMinSq`/`DistanceMaxSq`, −1 = no limit in 374 decisions; otherwise 9, 16, 25, 100, 144, 225 ..., i.e. 3 to 15 m) and validity flags: where the ped is (ValidOnFoot 336, ValidInCar 192, ValidOnBicycle 167, ValidInHeli 108) and conditions (NotValidInComplexScenario 49, ValidOnlyIfRandom 46, ValidOnlyIfSource 31, ValidOnlyIfSourceIsPlayer 30, ValidOnlyIfFriendlyWithTarget 21, crosswalk, interior, scenario radius, MP mission ped ...; 45 flags in all).
3. `eventDecisionMaker`: 25 decision makers, each a list of response-block names plus an optional parent: COP, Security, DEFAULT and GANG inherit from BASE; FAMILY from GANG; OFFDUTY_EMT from DEFAULT; Cat and Rabbit from SmallAnimal; PLAYER and EMPTY have none. Own response counts: DEFAULT 46, GANG 44, BASE 28, SWAT 26, FIREMAN 24, MEDIC 24, Security 22, COP 17, WildAnimal 16, DomesticAnimal 15, FAMILY 14, Cougar 13, DOG 12, SmallAnimal 12, HEN 7, HORSE 7, GULL 5, FISH 4, OFFDUTY_EMT 4, Shark 2, Rabbit 2, RAT 1, Cat 1.

Example: `DEFAULT_RESPONSE_GUN_AIMED_AT` lists THREAT (chance 0 when the player is the source), HANDS_UP (same), then GUN_AIMED_AT (chance 1 for the player). Our reading of the selection: the engine takes the ped's decision maker (plus its parent's blocks), finds the block for the event, and walks its decisions in order, taking the first whose flags fit the ped's state and distance and whose chance roll for the source type succeeds. The exact rule is not in the file. Status: mostly.

## ENT-025: how bird and fish groups spawn

They are ordinary population groups. `popgroups.ymt` (update) flags them: `birds_city` (A_C_Pigeon), `birds_countryside` (A_C_Cormorant, A_C_Chickenhawk, A_C_Crow) and `birds_ocean` (A_C_Seagull) are `POPGROUP_AMBIENT POPGROUP_AERIAL`; `birds_ground_city` (pigeon) and `birds_ground_ocean` (seagull) are `POPGROUP_AMBIENT POPGROUP_WILDLIFE`; `Fish` (hash 612920B7 = joaat "fish") and a second group CodeWalker names `fish_` both hold A_C_Fish and are `POPGROUP_AMBIENT POPGROUP_AQUATIC`. Only `Fish` is used in `popcycle.dat`. The file ends with `wildlifeHabitats`: sand_loose, sand_compact, sand_wet, grass_long, GRASS (hash 72623394), grass_short (our reading: the ground materials a WILDLIFE group may stand on).

In `popcycle.dat` (116 schedules, 12 two-hour slots, weekday and weekend) the groups sit in the `peds` list with a percentage weight like any other group, so they share the zone's `#Peds` cap: Fish in 21 schedules (weight 1 to 5, average 3.6), Birds_Countryside in 15 (average 54.5; ARMY has 79 against 29 for soldiers), Birds_Ocean 17 (28.7), Birds_City 12 (11.3, 0 at night), Birds_Ground_City 9 (16.0), Birds_Ground_Ocean 8 (22.5). Our reading: the AERIAL / AQUATIC / WILDLIFE flag tells the engine to place them in the air, in water or on a habitat material instead of on a pavement, and their high weight is why they fill many slots. Status: mostly (the placement rule is engine-side).
